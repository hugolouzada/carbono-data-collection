# carbono-data-collection
Analytics. Collecting data from carbon-credit offset projects, consolidation, AI enrichment, de-duplication.

Carbon credits are one of those nice ideas that look amazing on paper but are very hard to implement. Multiple sets of regulations to follow and quality levels to achieve, together with an ever-present risk of "double counting" of projects, have created a fragmented landscape of registries tracking offset projects. In this initiative, we will tackle part of this fragmentation through the consolidation, validation, and enrichment of the major data sources of carbon-credit projects. This consolidated data, and associated duplication analysis, will be made open-source to the community. We expect that this unified view of carbon-credit projects will reduce duplication, bring more transparency to the general public, and create a data foundation for future initiatives.

More details in [Carbono Data Collection Proposal](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1oJ6HrhUQk-tnXZ_qmPjUOO-UZnxVcKQRokCJQD-7j4c/edit?tab=t.0)



## MVP Description

The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) will validate the concept through the monitoring of Espírito Santo’s Programa Reflorestar—a state-level initiative that has restored 12,257.6 hectares of Atlantic Forest.
Objectives

Estimate the total carbon captured by the program since its inception using satellite imagery and literature-based sequestration rates. [Initial Report estimating the baseline]

Develop a model to monitor forest coverage changes annually.

Translate the results into estimated carbon credits with transparent quality tiers.


### Expected Outputs
A public dashboard visualizing forest recovery over time in Espírito Santo.

Annual and cumulative carbon sequestration estimates (≈120,000 tCO₂/year; ≈3.7 MtCO₂ over 30 years).

Simulated market value of the credits, estimated at US$2.9 million/year at US$24/tCO₂ (ARR BBB+ reference).


### Validation Path
Cross-check Reflorestar results with SOS Mata Atlântica and Pacto pela Restauração da Mata Atlântica data.

Compare predictions with known carbon sequestration benchmarks (9–11 tCO₂/ha/year).

Engage potential buyers or partners for voluntary carbon credit acquisition.


This MVP will serve as a proof of impact and technical validation, setting the stage for scaling to other Brazilian states or international programs.
