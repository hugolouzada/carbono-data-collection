# carbono-data-collection
Analytics. Collecting data from carbon-credit offset projects, consolidation, AI enrichment, de-duplication.

Carbon credits are one of those nice ideas that look amazing on paper but are very hard to implement. Multiple sets of regulations to follow and quality levels to achieve, together with an ever-present risk of "double counting" of projects, have created a fragmented landscape of registries tracking offset projects. In this initiative, we will tackle part of this fragmentation through the consolidation, validation, and enrichment of the major data sources of carbon-credit projects. This consolidated data, and associated duplication analysis, will be made open-source to the community. We expect that this unified view of carbon-credit projects will reduce duplication, bring more transparency to the general public, and create a data foundation for future initiatives.

More details in [Carbono Data Collection Proposal](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1oJ6HrhUQk-tnXZ_qmPjUOO-UZnxVcKQRokCJQD-7j4c/edit?tab=t.0)
